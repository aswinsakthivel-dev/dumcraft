Team Key & Rotation Guide

Your private team URL is protected by the secret TEAM_KEY environment variable.

To rotate the key: In your Cloudflare Pages dashboard, go to Settings → Environment variables, change TEAM_KEY to a new random value, and click Save. Then share the new link https://<site>/team/<NEW_KEY> with your team.
